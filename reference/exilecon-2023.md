# ExileCon 2023: Delve League edition

The card game has run at both earlier ExileCons, 2019 and 2023. The 2023 edition had the **Delve League** active. A GGG staff member who worked on the game has said that the **core rules are unchanged in 2026**. This page records what 2023 looked like, for context and to explain some player advice.

## Rules leaflet: 2023 vs 2026

Both leaflets share the same core rules: fighting staff in monster shirts, matching symbols, the number, the loadout and the two-Damage limit. The differences:

| Topic | 2023 (Delve League) | 2026 (Runes of Aldur League) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jewellery | Amulet and ring symbols can count toward Attack or Defence. | Same, and it now says explicitly that all of a piece's symbols go to the side you chose. |
| Crafting | Currency at the Crafting Bench on the top floor. | Currency at the Crafting Benches on the upper levels. **Each item can have only one Quality or Rune.** |
| Crafting tables | One, with very long queues | Three |
| Maps | Found by defeating Gods. Used at the Map Device against three random monsters. | Found from Act 4. Used at the Map Device against three random monsters. |
| League | Bring Sulphite to the Delve Wall and explore the Azurite Mine together. | Bring Logbooks to Expedition Island and explore together. |
| Monster shirts | Name, Attack, Defence | Name, Attack, Defence **and act** |
| Item bases | Path of Exile 1 bases | Path of Exile 2 bases |

## How staff ran fights in 2023

Players shared photos of the sheets staff carried. Each sheet listed:

- the staff member, their location and their monster,
- the monster's Attack and Defence,
- **loot instructions**, e.g. "Tier 5, 3 draw, 1 pick", or "special" for bosses,
- **durability instructions**, e.g. "clip all items" or a fixed order in which to clip items.

Each loot deck contained an **End of Deck** card meant for staff only. The first time a staff member drew it, they clipped it and shuffled it back in. The second time, they fetched a fresh loot deck.

## Delve

Delve fights were a gamble: you didn't know what you'd face until you were in. Like maps, a loss there cost you something (your Sulphite). Players saw it as risky compared with farming known monsters.

## Prizes

- 2019: the first five finishers got the last remaining DVDs of the game's alpha and some other items.
- 2023: mirror pins were the top prize, and supply was limited. Players also recall a pin for the first 50 people to beat the big bosses.
