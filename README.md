# ExileCon 2026 Card Game: Runes of Aldur League

A fan-written rules reference for the card game played at ExileCon 2026. It's based on the printed rules in the starter pack and on clarifications from GGG staff and experienced players. **It is not official.** If the staff at the venue tell you something different, they're right.

## The game in one minute

- **Your deck is your gear.** Your starter pack, from badge pickup, holds item cards: weapons, shields, armour and, later, jewellery. Each card has **symbols** such as Sword, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Life and Block.
- **Monsters are GGG staff in monster T-shirts.** Each shirt shows the monster's **Attack**, **Defence** and act.
- **To win a fight**, show
  - **Attack** (weapon symbols, plus any jewellery you assign to Attack) that meets the monster's **Defence**, and
  - **Defence** (armour and shield symbols, plus any jewellery you assign to Defence) that meets the monster's **Attack**.
- **Specific symbols must match exactly.** The **number** on a stat means that many *more* symbols of any kind.
- **Win, and your items take Damage** (staff clip durability) and you **pick new loot** from the staff member's deck. An item with two Damage is finished. **Lose, and nothing happens**: you fled.
- **Get stronger** by crafting with Currency at the Crafting Benches, turning in the quests on the back of your card box, running Maps from Act 4 and taking Logbooks to Expedition Island. Keep going until you can beat a **pinnacle boss** and win a mirror.

## How sure is this?

- Rules stated plainly come from the **printed leaflet and cards**, or from a **GGG staff member**.
- Paragraphs that start "players report" or "in 2023" come from players' experience and may not hold in 2026.
- Anything unresolved is listed under [FAQ → Open questions](faq.md#open-questions).
