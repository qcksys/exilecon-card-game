# FAQ

These are answers to common questions from players before ExileCon 2026. Where an answer comes from a player's memory rather than the printed rules or GGG staff, it says so.

## Getting started

**Where do I get my cards?**
In your swag bag at badge pickup. The starter pack includes the rules leaflet and a Quick Reference card.

**Are all starter packs the same?**
No. See [The starter pack](rules/equipment.md#the-starter-pack).

**Do I need to understand everything before I start?**
No. Each side of a fight is one counting check ([how to win](rules/fighting-monsters.md#how-to-win)). Crafting, maps and the league add depth later. Experienced players at the venue are generally happy to explain.

## Fighting

**How much damage does my weapon do? Nothing has an attack value.**
There's no hidden value. Every symbol on your weapon counts toward Attack. A card with two Swords gives two symbols of Attack. Those symbols can match a monster's specific Defence symbols or count toward its number.

**The monster shows Sword + Fire + 2. Do I need 2 or 4 symbols?**
Four: a Sword, a Fire and two more of anything. The number is *in addition to* the specific symbols ([worked examples](rules/fighting-monsters.md#worked-examples)).

**Can I split an amulet between Attack and Defence?**
No. All of its symbols go to one side. Your two rings can go to different sides, though.

**Can I use a shield with a two-handed weapon?**
No.

**Can I die or lose my cards?**
No. If you lose a fight you're assumed to have fled, and your cards only take Damage when you **win**. The exception is maps: losing in a map wastes the map, but not your gear.

**Can I fight Act 2 monsters before beating Act 1?**
Yes. There's no restriction on which monster you challenge or which order you do quests in. If your gear is good enough, go for it.

**Can I fight the same staff member again and again?**
No. Rotate between a few monsters instead.

**How do I find monsters of the right level?**
Their shirts now show their act. Monsters are spread around the venue, so on day 1 the queues give the early ones away.

## Crafting and progression

**Where do I craft?**
At the Crafting Benches on the upper levels. There are three in 2026. Players report two next to the Map Device on the 4th floor.

**How many crafts can one item take?**
Only one Quality *or* one Rune per item.

**Where do I hand in quests?**
At a Crafting Bench.

## Social

**Can I play and still see the panels?**
Yes. Play between panels. Just don't leave your cards lying around: a player's deck was stolen at a previous ExileCon. In that case GGG gave them a fresh starter pack.

**Can I trade cards with other players?**
The printed rules don't mention trading. In practice, players trade duplicates, pool cards in groups and hand their decks to friends.

**Can I get my cards signed?**
Yes, and people do. Talking to the developers you fight is part of the experience.

**How long does it take to reach a pinnacle boss?**
In 2023 one player reported about 7 hours of play, and said it got easier on day 2. There's no figure for 2026 yet.

## Open questions

Neither the printed rules nor anything said so far settles these. If you learn the answer, update the relevant page.

- **Block vs Life.** One player recalls that Sword and Block are generic ("no element") while Life acts like an element. What that means in practice isn't clear: the leaflet says every specific symbol must be matched by the same symbol.
- **Other symbols.** 2023 shirts showed a purple skull (probably Chaos). Is it on any 2026 cards?
- **Currency, Quality and Runes.** What each Currency item does, and what Quality and Runes add to an item.
- **Rematch rules.** How long before you can fight the same staff member again?
- **Logbooks and Expedition Island.** How you get Logbooks and what Expedition Island gives.
- **Map Device location.** The leaflet says main floor, a player says 4th floor.
- **Exceptional bases.** Are there "exceptional" item base cards?
- **Extra packs.** Can someone who accidentally received two starter packs use both? Asked, not answered.
