# Equipment

Before each fight you choose which items to use. Only those items count, and only those items can take Damage.

## Loadout limits

In one encounter you may use at most:

| Slot | Limit | Counts toward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon | 1 | Attack |
| Shield | 1, and only if your weapon is one-handed | Defence |
| Armour (body armour) | 1 | Defence |
| Amulet | 1 | Attack **or** Defence (your choice) |
| Ring | 2 | Attack **or** Defence (your choice, per ring) |

You don't have to fill every slot. Fight with whatever subset of your items you like.

## Where Attack and Defence come from

- **Attack** is the total of the symbols on your weapon, plus any jewellery you assign to Attack.
- **Defence** is the total of the symbols on your armour and shield, plus any jewellery you assign to Defence.

A weapon has no hidden base damage. Its Attack is exactly the symbols printed on it, including any symbols added by modifiers. A Shortsword (one Sword) gives one symbol of Attack. A Corroded Longsword (two Swords) gives two.

## Jewellery: all or nothing

You assign each amulet and each ring to either Attack or Defence. **All of that piece's symbols go to the side you chose.** You can't split one amulet between Attack and Defence. Different pieces can go to different sides. For example, one ring can count toward Attack while the other counts toward Defence.

## Two-handed weapons

A two-handed weapon takes up the shield slot as well, so you lose the shield's Defence. In exchange, two-handers tend to carry more symbols. See [how to tell a two-hander](cards-and-symbols.md#slot-icons-and-one--vs-two-handed-weapons) from the card's slot icons.

## Damaged items

Items lose durability when you **win** a fight ([details](fighting-monsters.md#after-the-fight)). Once an item has taken **two Damage**, it can no longer be used. Players' main advice is to keep cards in reserve and not spend durability on fights you don't need.

## The starter pack

You get your starter pack in your swag bag at badge pickup. It holds a card box with quests printed on the back, the rules leaflet, a Quick Reference card and your first item cards.

Starter packs are **not identical**. Players compared their packs on 6 November 2026 and reported:

- Six item cards per pack.
- A common pattern: a Shortsword, a Corroded Longsword, a Splintered Tower Shield and a Rusted Cuirass, plus a couple of other items.
- Magic or rare items seem to randomly *replace* a normal item, so a rare weapon could take the place of your only body armour.
- Most people got about one magic item. Some got all normal items, and a few got several rares.

Treat these as observations, not rules. The pack contents haven't been officially published.
