# Progression: loot, crafting, quests, maps and the league

Beating monsters gets you more items. This page covers everything else that makes your deck stronger and moves you toward the pinnacle bosses.

## Loot

Every staff member carries their own **loot deck**. When you beat them, they draw from it and you keep some of the cards (see [After the fight](fighting-monsters.md#after-the-fight)). Loot can include new items and **Currency**, and from Act 4 onward **Maps**.

In 2023 each loot deck ended with an "End of Deck" card. It told the staff member to start a fresh loot deck the second time they drew it. See [ExileCon 2023](../reference/exilecon-2023.md).

## Crafting

- Use **Currency Items** to upgrade your item cards at the **Crafting Benches** on the venue's upper levels.
- **Each item can have only one Quality or one Rune applied.** Rune is the league's mechanic; see below.
- There are **three crafting tables** at ExileCon 2026, up from one in 2023. Players report two of them next to the Map Device on the 4th floor. Expect queues anyway.
- In 2023, crafting results were stickers applied to the card itself.

> **Unconfirmed:** what each Currency item does, and exactly what Quality and Runes add, isn't covered by the printed rules. The crafting bench staff explain it when you get there.

## Quests

- Quests are printed on the **back of your card box**.
- When you complete one, **turn it in at a Crafting Bench** for extra rewards.
- You can do quests in any order.
- A player suggested that one Act 2 quest needs an Orb of Alchemy drop. Not confirmed.

## Maps

- From **Act 4** you can find **Maps**.
- Use a Map at the **Map Device** to face **three random monsters**, back to back.
- If you go in underprepared and lose, the map is **wasted**. Your gear isn't affected, so you can keep farming.

> **Conflicting locations:** the rules leaflet puts the Map Device on the *main floor*, but a player at the venue reported it on the *4th floor*, next to two of the crafting tables. Check the venue map on the day.

## The league: Runes of Aldur

ExileCon 2026 runs the **Runes of Aldur League**. Bring **Logbooks** to **Expedition Island** on the main floor, where players explore together to uncover valuable rewards.

How you get Logbooks and what Expedition Island rewards aren't described in the printed rules.

## Pinnacle bosses and the mirror prize

- There are **several pinnacle bosses**. In a pre-event interview, game director Jonathan Rogers said that staff members Octavian and Andrew are among them.
- The top prize is a **mirror**, a different design from 2023's.
- In 2023 the mirror prize was limited in number, and getting one took serious time. One player reported about 7 hours, and said it got easier on day 2.
- Players often pool cards in groups to reach the pinnacles. Some offer to carry others in exchange for their starter packs. This is a player arrangement, not a rule.
